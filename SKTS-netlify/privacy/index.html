YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

When you submit a project enquiry or service request, SK Technology Solutions receives the details you choose to share: your name, email, optional phone number and business name, project brief, selected services, and consent to project-related contact. If you submit a payment reference, we also receive your project / quote reference, reported amount, UPI transaction reference and payment date to match the transaction manually.

How we use your details

We use these details to respond to your request, discuss your project and prepare a quote. Selecting a service or submitting a form does not authorise a payment or create a confirmed order.

Where enquiries are processed

Our website uses Netlify Forms to receive, store and filter submissions for spam. Netlify processes this information on our behalf. Email notifications may include your enquiry details. Only enter the requested transaction reference and amount in the payment-reference form. Do not share your UPI PIN, bank password, card details, OTPs or sensitive documents.

Project portal accounts

The project portal uses Netlify Identity for sign-in and email confirmation. Project details, progress updates and payment-reference reviews are stored in Netlify Database. Customers can access records matching their confirmed account email; authorised admins can manage projects and payment reviews. Internal working notes are restricted to admins. The authentication library stores session information in your browser to keep you signed in. Portal data is not saved for offline use. Sign out on shared devices.

Local record keeping

When this website runs with our local backend, enquiries, project updates and payment-reference reviews are saved in a database on that computer. They are not automatically sent to Netlify or email. The local owner account uses a password hash and a session cookie, and authorised owners can export records for backup. Local records and accounts are separate from the hosted website.

Information saved on your device

The service shortlist is saved in your browser’s local storage so you can return to your selections. After a successful submission, we also save its reference number, submission type and date on this browser so you can find your receipt. These receipts do not contain your name, email, reported amount or UPI transaction details, and do not show live project or verified payment status. You can clear them using “Clear references from this browser”. Other form details are not saved to local storage. The installable website caches public pages and assets for offline access; submitting enquiries requires an internet connection.

WhatsApp payment reminders

If WhatsApp reminders are enabled with your opt-in, we use Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform to send your project reference, agreed budget, verified payment total, amount due and due date to your chosen WhatsApp number. We store delivery status and opt-out history. You can stop reminders from your portal or reply STOP to a reminder. Local client invitations and passwords are separate from public website accounts.

Your choices
