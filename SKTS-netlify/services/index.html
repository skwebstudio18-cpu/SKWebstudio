BEFORE WE START

A little more clarity.

Useful answers about choosing services, project scope and working with SKTS.

Are the listed prices the final project cost?

They are starting prices for the listed service scope. Your written quote confirms the final scope, applicable taxes and any additional costs. Selecting services creates a shortlist and does not place an order.

Does the app starting price include a native Android or iOS app?

The listed Mobile app experiences package is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA). Native Android and iOS development, and digital branding, need a custom quote based on your requirements.

Are hosting, a domain and third-party tools included?

Hosting and third-party fees are quoted separately. We will confirm the deployment scope and any domain or service requirements in your written quote.

How long will my project take?

The schedule depends on the agreed scope, required content and review steps. We confirm delivery dates for your project rather than promising one timeline for every service.

How can I follow progress and share documents?

Invited clients can sign in to their private portal to view service milestones, agreed budgets, verified payments and project files. Documents and review comments stay linked to the project.

How do payments and ongoing support work?

Pay only against an agreed quote using the payment details provided. Submit the transaction reference so our team can verify the bank receipt. Website care starts at the listed monthly price; the maintenance scope is confirmed separately, and selecting it does not enable automatic renewal.
